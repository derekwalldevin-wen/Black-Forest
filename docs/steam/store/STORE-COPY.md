## 中文短描述

提起灯，走进黑森林与山海之墟。在单人回合制卡牌冒险中选择传承，观察敌人意图，权衡攻击、格挡与资源。沿途挑选卡牌、订立妖契，在事件和商店中塑造牌组，寻找穿过迷雾的道路。

## English short description

Carry your lantern through a dark forest and the ruins of Shanhai. Choose a starting deck, read enemy intents, and balance attacks, block and resources in a solo turn-based card adventure. Shape your deck through card rewards, pacts, events and shops.

## 详细介绍草稿

**先看意图，再决定出牌。** 敌人的下一步可见。把行动力用于攻击还是格挡，是否值得付出生命抽牌，何时积累灵韵连打符箓，都由你决定。

**三套传承，两片境域。** 猎血、守御和符咒提供不同初始牌组。旅途从黑森林延伸到山海之墟，穿过六章十八场遭遇。

**拿牌并非唯一答案。** 战后选择适合牌组的卡，也可以跳过；商店提供补给与删牌，妖契与事件带来新的取舍。

**为阅读与选择留出空间。** 竖屏战斗构图配合桌面阅读栏，完整卡牌说明、敌人意图和状态规则可随时查看。提供中文与英文预览、独立音乐/音效音量、大字手牌和减少动态设置。

进度保存在本机检查点，关闭游戏后可从最近保存处继续；不承诺任意战斗瞬间续战或跨设备云同步。

## English detailed outline

**Read the intent, then commit.** Decide how to spend Energy on attack and block, when to trade health for cards, and when to save Spirit for a chain of talismans.

**Three starting decks, two realms.** Blood, Guard and Talisman offer distinct starting decks. Travel through six chapters and eighteen encounters, from the forest to the ruins of Shanhai.

**A card reward is a choice.** Take a card that supports your plan or skip it. Shops, pacts and events shape the deck you carry forward.

**Keep the rules within reach.** The portrait battle stage is accompanied by reading panels on wider desktop windows. Inspect full card rules, enemy intents and ongoing status effects. Chinese and an English preview are available, alongside music and sound controls, a large-text hand reader and reduced-motion settings.

Progress uses local checkpoints. Resuming returns to the latest saved checkpoint, rather than restoring an arbitrary moment during combat.
